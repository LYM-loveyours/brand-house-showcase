Perspective / DESIGN STUDY

A shared language. Five distinct voices.

A family of identities works best when the system is strong enough to hold difference. In this study, the shared grid, typography and navigation provide continuity. Each label then finds its own pace through image, colour and language.

The aim is recognition without repetition. A visitor can move between labels without relearning the interface, while still feeling the change in character.
