Why good design needs room to breathe.

Whitespace is part of the structure. It gives a headline its scale, separates one idea from the next and lets an image hold attention without competing with every other element.

On smaller screens, that principle stays the same. Columns stack, type scales and interactions remain direct. The hierarchy survives because it was designed before decoration.
