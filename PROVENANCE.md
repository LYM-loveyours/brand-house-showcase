Only six neutral imagery files were copied. The prior studio asset ledger identifies these as LYM-generated atmosphere, with no marks, text or people, reviewed in its original production process. This is evidence of recorded origin, not an independent legal clearance or a grant of reuse rights to visitors. The original client photography and brand artwork were excluded.

Fonts were copied from the existing local font library with their OFL notices. Fictional label names are illustrative creative choices, not trademark clearance. No registered-trademark claim is made.

No repository history, original integrations, enquiry endpoints, client details, deployment settings or secrets were imported. These exclusions are part of the local release review; publication remains a separate approval.
