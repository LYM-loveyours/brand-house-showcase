THE HOUSE / A DESIGN STUDY

Character first.
Everything follows.

Forme House is a fictional brand-house experience designed and developed by Love Yours Media.

THE APPROACH

One structure.
Many expressions.

Editorial confidence, built on a useful foundation.

This study brings together a responsive grid, locally hosted typography, considered motion and five distinct fictional identities. The layouts adapt to the content rather than repeating the same arrangement on every page.

The names, labels and editorial stories are illustrative. Forme House is not a trading business. No affiliation with any real brand is intended, and no commercial outcomes or credentials are claimed.

01

Clarity

Strong hierarchy and simple journeys, from the index to each label.

02

Character
